# Pulse

> Concentric expanding rings synced to beat with light feedback trails

- type: shader
- pace param: expansion_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.5 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.082 default=0.088 hi=0.097
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.143 / 0.238
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A dartboard dead-center in frame: nested concentric rings streaming outward from a small
core. Quiet half: a few broad soft bands — sky-blue disc, deep crimson ring, gold band,
green outer rings — separated by wide black gaps, like a giant iris or sonar screen. Loud
half: dozens of tight rings in magenta, green, teal, and copper packing the frame edge to
edge, corners still black.

## Motion character
The most kinetic of this group (~0.088 loud): continuous radial displacement, rings
visibly traveling outward. expansion_speed reads exactly as it sounds — lo (0.082)
confines softer rings to a centered circle with a wide black margin; hi (0.097) packs
thinner, more numerous rings wall-to-wall. Displacement, with beat-flash accents.

## Energy response
Loud brightens 0.14 → 0.24 and multiplies the ring count: the quiet half is a few slow
wide bands, the loud half a dense strobing bullseye. Documented response: beat phase
drives expansion, kicks fire accent rings, bass thickens them, beats flash the center.

## Palette
Saturated rainbow bands on black — blue/crimson/gold/green in the quiet half shifting to
magenta/green/teal when loud. Dark field, vivid rings; mid-low average brightness.

## Casting notes
Hypnotic tunnel, sonar ping, target, ripples-on-water, trance-lock moods; the obvious
choice for four-on-floor sections since the rings are beat-synced. Always centered and
perfectly symmetric — cannot do asymmetry, texture, or literal imagery. Works solo
full-frame, or as a center anchor behind/under an off-center foreground element.
