# Helix

> The last eight seconds of audio swept into a twisting ribbon you fly along — the cross-section is the spectrum, the colour is the timbre, and every beat stays visible in the wall as it recedes.

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0451
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.177 / 0.176
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A soft-focus tunnel of tangled glowing threads — flying down the bore of a nest of teal yarn, or a fiber-optic vortex. Concentric skeins of filament wrap the tunnel walls; a dark cavity marks the vanishing point and drifts off-center. Everything is blurred to gauze; individual fibers streak and cross rather than resolving sharply.

## Motion character
The most mobile of this set at default: 0.029 quiet, 0.045 loud (moderate on the catalog scale). Reads as continuous forward flight plus rotational churn of the thread walls — displacement, not flicker. In the loud half the tangle thickens and swirls harder around a smaller bore.

## Energy response
Brightness is flat across halves (luma 0.177 vs 0.176) — energy shows as movement and density, not light: motion +55% loud, the tunnel wall closing in with denser fiber. Per the shipped description the wall is the recent spectrum, so beats persist as structure receding behind you.

## Palette
Monochrome teal-to-green in this render — cyan-teal skeins quiet, deeper forest green loud. Mid-level luma (~0.18), soft low-saturation glow; the only black is the bore.

## Casting notes
Wormholes, hyperspace, undersea kelp drift, dream descent — anything "traveling through". Full-frame environment layer, not an accent. Cannot be still or graphic; permanently soft-focus, so no crisp geometry. The render stays green despite a documented timbre-hue input — do not cast it for color variety on this evidence.
