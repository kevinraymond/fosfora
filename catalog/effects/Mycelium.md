# Mycelium

> Swarming tendrils — leader particles follow curl noise flow fields, depositing follower particles that form spring-connected chains. Chains branch probabilistically on audio onset, creating a living mycelial network that grows at tips and decays at roots

- type: feedback
- pace param: curl_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.35 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.00966 default=0.0095 hi=0.00993
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.0447 / 0.1314
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
Glowing seafoam-green filaments tangled across a pure black field — the
veins of a leaf, hyphae under UV light, or green marble veining that
luminesces. Quiet half: a sparse wiry web, thin threads with dark voids
between them. Loud half: the network thickens into a dense luminous mat —
bright braided trunks, fine hair-like branches, and a scatter of small
glowing dots (spore-like nodes) filling the gaps. Feedback trails give every
filament a soft phosphor smear.

## Motion character
Slow creep at default: ~0.0095 mean Δ on the loud half reads as tips
crawling forward and the whole web slowly writhing — churn along fixed
strands, no jumps, no flicker. The curl_speed sweep is imperceptible in the
numbers (0.0097 / 0.0095 / 0.0099 lo/default/hi) and the lo/hi stills look
alike; do not cast the sweep expecting a visible tempo change.

## Energy response
Strong density response: luma triples quiet-to-loud (0.045 → 0.131) and
motion triples (0.0030 → 0.0095) as onsets trigger branching and bass drives
tip growth. Quiet = a few skeletal threads in blackness; loud = a
frame-filling bioluminescent network. Reads as growth, not brightness
pumping.

## Palette
Monochrome green: seafoam, mint and emerald filaments with white-hot cores
on true black. High local contrast, saturated, with occasional faint yellow
glints along strands.

## Casting notes
Casts as fungal networks, neurons, root systems, cave bioluminescence,
circuit-board veins. Pure black ground makes it a first-rate overlay layer.
Suits organic-growth builds, eerie biology, deep-forest or abyssal moods.
Cannot do geometry, symmetry, or beat-locked strobe; its response is
cumulative — the net on screen is the last minute of music. Pairs well
under Morph's speckle or behind Lattice Shells' warm centerpiece.
