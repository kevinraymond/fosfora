# Lattice Brain

> 3D cellular automata — gyri-and-sulci folds wrapping the whole volume (S6-10/B5-7/12/M). Bass drives the evolution; the surface relief shifts and reorganises.

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0048
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.088 / 0.117
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
One glowing orb centered on a dark navy void. Quiet half: a fuzzy cyan puff with a rounded-diamond silhouette, nearly featureless. Loud half: the puff resolves into a moon-sized sphere with real surface relief — pale ice-blue walnut lobes first, then a turquoise ball carved with a spiral groove, like a storm eye or a glowing marble with a fingerprint.

## Motion character
Near-static quiet (0.00045) — the orb just breathes. The loud half lifts it to a slow, visible reorganisation (0.0048): lobes and grooves migrate across the surface. Growth and relief-shift, not displacement; the object never leaves screen center.

## Energy response
Loud half brightens the orb ~33% (luma 0.088 → 0.117), doubles its contrast (luma_std 0.061 → 0.122), and raises motion ~11× — the featureless puff becomes a sculpted, folded sphere. Bass sets the evolution rate; onsets seed new growth; brilliance extends structure lifetimes.

## Palette
Cyan / turquoise / ice-blue monochrome on deep navy-black. Dim-to-moderate; soft volumetric glow, no hard edges.

## Casting notes
A brain, a moon, an egg, a planet, a thinking machine — single contemplative centerpiece imagery. Slow by nature; cannot strobe, travel, or fill the frame. Strong as the focal object over a subtle background layer in ambient or brooding passages. The quiet-half version is almost featureless, so save the detail moments for loud material.
