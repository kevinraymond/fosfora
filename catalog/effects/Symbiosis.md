# Symbiosis

> Particle life — multi-species emergent ecosystems, crystals, and predator-prey dynamics from an asymmetric force matrix

- type: particle
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0198
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.073 / 0.077
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A dark violet-black void scattered with dozens of small glittering colonies —
loose clumps of pinprick particles in mixed colors (magenta, green, cyan, orange,
white) that read like bacterial cultures in a petri dish, or drifting plankton
swarms seen in blacklight. In the quiet half one colony curls into a spiral —
an orange-and-blue whorl like a tiny galaxy. In the loud half the colonies
stretch into comet streaks with hot white cores and colored tails, all smearing
in a shared direction as the swarms travel. Individual particles carry RGB
chromatic fringes, giving the clusters a shimmering confetti sparkle.

## Motion character
Drift and swarm: colonies wander, orbit each other, merge and split — pace reads
as displacement of many small bodies, with fine per-particle shimmer on top.
Moderate overall (quiet 0.0128, loud 0.0198). No pace sweep was rendered; the
force matrix, not a speed dial, sets the tempo.

## Energy response
Subtle in brightness (luma 0.073 → 0.077 — it stays dark), clearer in behavior:
motion rises ~1.5x and the stills change character from milling clusters and a
spiral to directed comet-streak migration with brighter white cores. Energy shows
up as agitation and travel, not as light.

## Palette
Near-black violet ground with small saturated accents: magenta, spring green,
cyan, amber, white-hot cores. Very low mean value, high per-pixel saturation —
jewel dust on black.

## Casting notes
Microscopic and biological moods: cultures, plankton, fireflies, star clusters,
emergent "life finds a way" passages. Its dimness (luma ~0.07) makes it a
natural overlay or additive layer above darker backgrounds; solo it reads as an
intimate, small-scale scene rather than a wall of light. It cannot fill the
frame, flash, or hit hard on a beat — no strobe, no big shapes — so do not cast
it as a drop moment; cast it for patience, texture, and close-up wonder.
