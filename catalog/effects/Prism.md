# Prism

> Kaleidoscopic N-fold mirror symmetry over FBM and geometric patterns

- type: shader
- pace param: rotation_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.3 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.069 default=0.069 hi=0.070
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.442 / 0.546
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A full-bleed kaleidoscope mandala: an eight-petal rosette at dead center, ringed by nested
bands of felted, watercolor-soft noise mirrored into perfect radial symmetry. Tie-dye
texture throughout — no hard edges, no black anywhere, wall-to-wall color like a spun
silk scarf under a projector.

## Motion character
Constant full-frame churn (~0.07): the whole mandala kneads and rotates at once. The
rotation_speed sweep barely registers in the loud half (0.0688 → 0.0696; quiet half
0.060 → 0.070) and the stills are near-identical — pace reads as a slightly faster swirl
of the same picture, never a different one. Churn, not displacement or flicker.

## Energy response
The loud half lifts brightness 0.44 → 0.55 and flattens contrast (luma_std 0.127 → 0.105):
colors wash toward pastel teal and bubblegum pink, the rosette center goes mustard-gold.
The quiet half is hotter and deeper — magenta, saffron yellow, a dark-pink rosette on
teal. Documented response: bass pulses zoom, beat flashes the center, centroid steers the
palette.

## Palette
Hot magenta, bubblegum pink, teal, mustard gold, mint accents. High saturation, high
value, zero black — one of the brightest full-frame sources in the catalog.

## Casting notes
Psychedelic, festival, 1960s liquid-light-show moods; mandala/stained-rose-window imagery.
Always busy, always bright, always radially symmetric — it cannot do dark, minimal,
asymmetric, or literal. Strong solo wallpaper for peak sections, or a base layer under a
dark silhouetted foreground.
