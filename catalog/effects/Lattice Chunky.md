# Lattice Chunky

> 3D cellular automata — bold, cavity-riddled lobes with a ragged, non-spherical silhouette (S6-9/B6-8/14/M). Bass drives the churn; the per-cell lifetime keeps it hollow.

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0037
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.084 / 0.103
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A chartreuse cauliflower suspended center-frame on dark navy. Quiet half: a ragged off-center cluster of moss-yellow florets, like broccoli crowns or lichen clumps. Loud half: a fuller round ball of yellow-green lobes riddled with black cavities and gaps — the silhouette stays ragged, never a clean sphere.

## Motion character
Near-static quiet (0.00038); the loud half churns slowly (0.0037) — lobes swell, split, and hollow out in place. Growth and churn, no displacement, no flicker.

## Energy response
Loud half brightens ~23% (luma 0.084 → 0.103), doubles contrast (luma_std 0.049 → 0.098), and lifts motion ~10×: sparse clumps consolidate into a bigger, brighter, more cavernous ball. Bass drives the churn rate; onsets inject seed clusters.

## Palette
Chartreuse / moss / yellow-green monochrome on deep navy-black. Dim-to-moderate glow; matte and organic.

## Casting notes
Moss, lichen, a broccoli planet, a sulfur bloom, a toxic cloud — organic-mass imagery with an acid tint. A single centered object; slow; cannot strobe or travel. The yellow-green reads distinctly against the cyan and violet of its lattice siblings — the pick when a scene needs an acidic or sickly note.
