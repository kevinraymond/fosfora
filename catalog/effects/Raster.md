# Raster

> Video wall — particles map to image pixels with audio-driven displacement and spring-return physics

- type: particle
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.051
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.159 / 0.208
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A churning cloud of TV-static confetti — white, magenta, and green micro-particles with
warm amber patches at the core — floating as a rough disc on black. Early quiet frames
show a thin glitter band hanging like spray over a horizon line; later frames a round,
brain-like boiling mass. In these renders the source image never settles into anything
legible: it stays a sandstorm of pixels for the whole track.

## Motion character
Vigorous continuous churn even in the quiet half (0.036), rising to 0.051 loud with
horizontal streaks flying off the mass. Reads as displacement plus per-particle scatter —
a swarm being blown around, not flicker. No pace sweep was rendered.

## Energy response
Loud brightens 0.16 → 0.21 and smears the cloud sideways (motion 0.036 → 0.051); the quiet
half is a more compact, dimmer cloud. Documented response: bass drives shard/swirl/radial
displacement, onsets scatter individual particles, kicks multiply the burst force.

## Palette
White sparkle with RGB confetti fringing, amber-brown core patches, black field. High
local contrast (luma_std 0.23–0.26): bright grain on dark, like analog noise.

## Casting notes
BYO-media effect, and flag: in this catalog render the test image is blasted into static
and never reads — treat it as an image-dissolver, not an image-displayer. Cast for pixel
storms, signal decay, dissolution-of-a-picture moods, glitter nebulae. If the screenplay
needs the source image recognizable, these renders give no evidence it will be. Pairs as
a chaotic mid-ground over a stable backdrop.
