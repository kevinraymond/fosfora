# Tesla

> Charged particles spiral through magnetic dipole fields — interweaving helical trajectories with audio-reactive field strength and polarity flips

- type: particle
- pace param: field_rotation (lo=0.15 default=0.3 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.0134 default=0.0134 hi=0.0137
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.066 / 0.082
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A black void holding two large soft glows — twin powder-blue nebula lobes, like
a pair of flashlight beams diffused through fog, separated by a dark seam. These
are the dipole's poles; their axis sits at different tilts frame to frame
(diagonal, side-by-side, mirrored above/below a dark midline in the quiet half).
Between and inside the lobes drift sparse pinprick sparks in pink, green, and
cyan. At this render's exposure the advertised helical trails do NOT read — the
image is dominated by the two blurry glows, with the particles as faint glitter,
not visible spirals.

## Motion character
Slow and nebular: the lobes swell and their axis leans over seconds — pace reads
as gentle drift, not churn or flicker. Motion is low (quiet 0.0073, loud 0.0134,
p95 0.045). The field_rotation sweep is imperceptible in the numbers (loud mean
0.0134 / 0.0134 / 0.0137 for lo/default/hi) and the stills differ only in which
way the axis happens to point; do not cast lo-vs-hi expecting a visible tempo
change.

## Energy response
Modest: loud roughly doubles motion (0.0073 → 0.0134) and lifts luma 0.066 →
0.082 — the lobes brighten and more colored sparks appear inside them. No strobe,
no burst; the loud half reads as a stronger, more populated glow, not an event.

## Palette
Cold blue-white glows on black, with tiny saturated accents of magenta, green,
and cyan. Very dark overall (luma 0.07–0.08) but high local contrast — bright
soft cores against true black.

## Casting notes
Cosmic and scientific-sublime moods: twin suns, aurora cores, energy contained
in a dark chamber. Its black surround and soft additive glows make it an easy
mixer over or under other dark effects. It cannot deliver crisp structure — no
lines, arcs, or legible helices at this exposure — nor fast beat response, so
cast it for atmosphere and slow builds, not percussion. If a scene needs visible
spirals, verify at app exposure first; these stills show glow, not geometry.
