# Lattice Builder

> 3D cellular automata — a churning, cavity-riddled organism that builds and dissolves lobed folds (S2,6,9/B4,6,8-9/10/M). Bass drives the evolution; the grid freezes in silence.

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0024
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.083 / 0.089
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A single dim lavender-violet growth centered on near-black navy. Quiet half: a clumpy wreath of cauliflower lobes around a dark central cavity — a smoke-ring of lilac florets. Loud half: the wreath fills into a rounder, denser cluster of periwinkle lobes with black gaps between them.

## Motion character
Near-frozen quiet (0.00041), a slow churn loud (0.0024) — lobes accrete and dissolve in place. Reads as growth, not displacement or flicker; among the slowest movers in the catalog even in its loud half.

## Energy response
Modest: luma 0.083 → 0.089, motion up ~6×. Music mainly changes structure — the hollow ring of the quiet half packs into a solid cluster loud, with more surface contrast (luma_std 0.035 → 0.049). Bass sets the generation rate; onsets seed clusters.

## Palette
Lavender, lilac, periwinkle on dark navy. Dim, desaturated, soft — the darkest and gentlest of the lattice-family renders.

## Casting notes
Storm cloud, lilac coral, ash bloom, an explosion frozen mid-burst. A quiet centerpiece for subdued or melancholy passages. Cannot flash, travel, or brighten much (luma stays ~0.09); too dim to carry a peak moment alone — layer it with something that brings light.
