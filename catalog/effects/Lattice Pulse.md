# Lattice Pulse

> 3D cellular automata — Von Neumann oscillating structures (S2-3/B3/2/VN). Bass drives the evolution; the grid freezes in silence.

- type: particle
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.00114
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.0781 / 0.0798
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A near-empty midnight-navy field with a tiny loose cluster of glowing teal
specks at dead center — like a handful of cyan diatoms or frost crystals seen
through a microscope, occupying maybe a tenth of the frame. In the quiet half
there are only three or four isolated flecks; in the loud half they multiply
into a sparse, granular constellation of small cyan cell-clumps, still
surrounded by vast dark emptiness.

## Motion character
Essentially static. The quiet half measures motion 0.00003 — a frozen frame —
and even the loud half only reaches 0.00114, which reads as slow cellular
churn: individual specks blinking in and out rather than anything traveling.
There is no pace param. This is growth-by-stillness, not movement.

## Energy response
The music changes population, not brightness: luma barely moves (0.078 →
0.080) but motion jumps ~38x from quiet to loud as bass drives generations.
Quiet = a dead, frozen scatter of flecks; loud = the cluster visibly breathing
and speckling, though it never grows large or bright.

## Palette
Two colors only: deep desaturated navy-charcoal background and small saturated
teal/cyan points. Extremely low value range (luma_std 0.009–0.012) — the frame
is almost uniformly dark.

## Casting notes
FLAG: renders are near-black and very sparse — a tiny centered speck-cluster
in a huge dark field. Cannot carry a scene alone at these settings. Plays as a
faint background organism, a "signs of life in the void" accent, or an overlay
garnish behind a brighter layer. Suits microscopic, cryptic, deep-space or
bioluminescent-plankton moods at whisper intensity. Cannot do impact, fills,
or brightness; pair with a luminous base layer (e.g. Lumen, Mycelium).
