# Morph

> Shape morphing — particles spring between images and geometry on beat drops

- type: particle
- pace param: none swept (spring_k, turbulence etc. exist but no lo/hi renders)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0135 (quiet half is higher: 0.0267)
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.1671 / 0.1198
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A huge ellipse built from hundreds of thousands of pin-prick particles on a
black-violet void — a dinner plate or lens seen in perspective, its surface
alive with fine moiré interference ripples like an op-art print or a CRT
close-up. Particle speckle carries RGB fringing, so the white mass shimmers
with green and magenta confetti. Between targets the shape detonates: one
loud-half still is a radial starburst of confetti sprayed from a small
glowing white core, mid-flight between geometries.

## Motion character
Counterintuitive: measured motion is HIGHER in the quiet half (0.0267) than
the loud (0.0135) — the pad keeps the assembled plate shimmering with moiré
crawl (flicker/churn across a held shape), while the loud-half stills caught
settled or slowly-reforming targets. Peaks (p95 0.075 quiet / 0.034 loud)
come from the explosive scatter transitions. Reads as churn-on-a-surface
punctuated by displacement bursts, not steady drift. No pace sweep rendered.

## Energy response
Onsets trigger morphs to the next target, so the loud half spends time
exploded into dim scattered confetti (luma drops 0.167 → 0.120) while the
quiet half holds a bright assembled disc. Energy shows as shape-shattering
and reassembly, not as brightness gain.

## Palette
Near-monochrome: white/pale-violet particle mass on a black-purple vignette,
with strong green and magenta chromatic speckle at particle scale. High
contrast, glitchy-neutral saturation.

## Casting notes
Casts as op-art, CRT static sculpture, particle disintegration, a "form
assembling from dust" beat-drop moment. Its currency is the explode/reform
cycle — great for drops and transitions, and as a monochrome graphic
centerpiece. The dense moiré shimmers constantly, which can read as noise on
sustained quiet passages. Nearly colorless at default, so it layers safely
over any palette; pair with a colored bed (Lumen, Mycelium) for hue.
