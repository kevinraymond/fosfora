# Genesis

> Multi-species Particle Lenia — two species with complementary kernels self-organize into predator/prey, symbiosis, or competition dynamics

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0082
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.069 / 0.079
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
Petri-dish colonies on a black field: ragged amoeba-shaped clusters of pinpoint dots, each ringed by a mint-teal dotted membrane, edged with coral-orange speckles, filled with a dim magenta stipple. The quiet half shows nine or ten separate colonies in a loose scatter, one a small hollow teal ring; by the loud half they have fused into one sprawling dotted continent across the lower frame with satellite islands above.

## Motion character
Slow protoplasmic drift — colonies quiver at their edges and creep together (motion 0.006–0.008, well below the ~0.03 moderate mark). Reads as cell-culture time-lapse: boundaries dissolving and reforming, no displacement across the frame, no flicker.

## Energy response
Loud half raises motion ~34% (0.0061 → 0.0082) and luma slightly (0.069 → 0.079); the visible change is structural — separate colonies quiet, one merged mass with orange edge-spray loud. Documented mappings: beats drop new seeds of alternating species, onsets kick a destabilizing impulse, bass expands/contracts the organisms.

## Palette
Mint-teal outlines, coral-orange speckle, magenta interiors on near-pure black. Low overall brightness (luma ~0.07); saturated pointillist accents rather than washes.

## Casting notes
Microscopy, alien cultures, night coral, living maps, dyed microbe slides. Intimate and organic — never architectural, never fast; it cannot flood the frame with light or hit hard on a beat. Works as a dark-background foreground layer for slow, biological, or uneasy passages. Not for strobe or impact moments.
