In the quiet half it is a wide swirling disc — a galaxy seen at a shallow tilt, ivory-white core feathering out through olive-gold and sea-green dust arms, edges dissolving into grain. In the loud half it tightens into a thin looping filament: a gold thread traced into a butterfly/figure-eight loop around a glowing white-green eye, and by the late loud half it has contracted to a tiny bright ellipse in an otherwise empty black frame.
