# Chromatica

> Chord mandala: twelve orbital rings on the circle of fifths bloom with the chord being played, the whole palette tinted by the song's detected key. A Consonance toggle draws tension lines between clashing notes.

- type: shader
- pace param: rotation_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.3 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.031 default=0.032 hi=0.037
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.152 / 0.250
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A centered mandala on a solid royal-purple field. Nested concentric hoops — mint-green and hot-magenta halos — ring a straw-gold string-art web: straight chords strung between a dozen glowing beads (peach, lilac, aqua) arranged on an inner circle, forming star-polygon lattices. A fuchsia glow burns at the hub. Reads as a rose window, an astrolabe, or harmonic string art.

## Motion character
Slow stately rotation of the whole wheel while rings breathe and the chord web redraws; motion reads as smooth angular displacement plus soft pulse, never flicker. The rotation sweep is subtle on camera: loud-half motion goes 0.031 → 0.037 from lo to hi, so hi spins visibly faster but the composition is unchanged; most measured motion comes from ring blooming, not spin.

## Energy response
Loud half brightens markedly (luma 0.152 → 0.250) and more than doubles motion (0.0145 → 0.0325, p95 0.082): outer hoops multiply, the chord web gets denser and brighter, and the whole wheel pumps with the kick. Quiet half is a sparser, calmer wheel — fewer rings, thinner web — but still fully present and colorful.

## Palette
Saturated purple/magenta ground — the frame is never dark. Mint/seafoam hoops, magenta rings, pale gold web lines, fuchsia core, candy-colored node beads. High saturation, mid-high value throughout.

## Casting notes
Sacred geometry, planetarium charts, kaleidoscope altars, music-theory-made-visible; anything wanting a bright ornamental centerpiece. Always a centered radial composition on an opaque purple ground — it cannot do darkness, asymmetry, or full-frame texture, and it will not sit quietly behind anything. Cast it as the featured subject; its documented chroma/key mappings make it the natural pick for harmonically rich, melodic material.
