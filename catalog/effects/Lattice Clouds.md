# Lattice Clouds

> 3D cellular automata — expanding cumulus-like structures (S13-26/B13-14,17-19/2/M). Bass drives the evolution; the grid freezes in silence.

- type: particle
- pace param: none (only `default` rendered)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.0055
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.131 / 0.128
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A glowing slab of billowing puffs — the cubic volume seen face-on as a tilted trapezoidal block, filled edge to edge with soft celadon-to-chartreuse cumulus lobes and punched through with black holes. Reads as a window into a cloudbank, or a block of glowing foam; the block's straight edges are plainly visible against the navy void.

## Motion character
The liveliest lattice preset, though still slow: 0.0023 quiet, 0.0055 loud. Puffs billow and holes open and close inside the block — churn in place, no displacement. The quiet half already visibly simmers rather than freezing solid.

## Energy response
Brightness is flat (luma 0.131 vs 0.128 — the brightest of the lattice family) while motion more than doubles loud; the block also grows more perforated and shifts greener in the loud half. Energy reads as agitation, not light. Bass sets the evolution rate; onsets seed clusters.

## Palette
Pale celadon, cream-green, chartreuse on dark navy. Moderate brightness, soft nebular glow, monochrome green.

## Casting notes
Cloudbanks, foam, glowing reef, dream fog, a contained storm. Its visible straight block edges break the illusion of open sky — cast it as "clouds in a vitrine or window", not a skyscape. Slow and constant-brightness, so no strobe or beat hits; a good mid-layer mass for ambient or slowly building sections.
