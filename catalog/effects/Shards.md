# Shards

> Animated Voronoi cells with glowing fracture edges

- type: shader
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.063
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.524 / 0.576
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A wall-to-wall stained-glass mosaic: irregular polygonal panes grouted by glowing white
mortar lines with a faint prismatic fringe. Quiet half: panes in brick red, navy, mustard,
plum, and slate on a frosted near-white ground, many cells washed pale. Loud half: the
same mosaic flipped into greens, magenta, and olive with the fills more fully colored.
No focal subject — an even tiled field edge to edge.

## Motion character
Mean motion 0.063 loud but p95 hits 0.167 — the frame mostly holds, then jumps. Reads as
percussive flicker and shatter (edges flashing, cells displacing on hits), not continuous
churn. No pace sweep was rendered; documented response says onsets drive the shatter
displacement.

## Energy response
Loud nearly doubles motion (0.034 → 0.063) and rewrites the entire palette — warm
red/navy/mustard to green/magenta/olive (centroid-steered) — while brightness rises only
modestly, 0.52 → 0.58. The frame is bright in both halves; the music changes color and
violence, not luminance.

## Palette
Jewel tones in white glowing grout: brick red, navy, mustard, plum quiet; green, magenta,
olive loud. Bright overall (luma ~0.55), mid-high saturation, high cell-to-cell contrast.

## Casting notes
Stained glass, cathedral windows, cracked ice, crystalline fracture, mosaic-floor moods —
best on percussive material where the shatter-on-onset shows. Always full-frame tiled and
bright: it cannot do darkness, empty space, or a focal subject. Cast as a beat-reactive
wallpaper or a bright base layer under a dark centered element (a Reliquary figure over
Shards is a natural pairing).
