# Flux

> Organic smoke — particles follow 3D curl noise flow field with audio-reactive turbulence

- type: particle
- pace param: flow_intensity (lo=0.15 default=0.5 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.091 default=0.092 hi=0.091
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.380 / 0.398
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A wall-to-wall seething field of hot grain — millions of orange, red, and gold specks packed edge to edge, faintly smeared radially from center like embers caught in an updraft, or the boiling surface of a sun. In the loud half, pale ghost-streaks flicker at the frame edges. No figure, no negative space: the texture IS the image. Despite the shipped "smoke" description, on this track it renders as ember-static, not wisps.

## Motion character
The most agitated effect in this batch: mean Δ 0.080 quiet and 0.092 loud (p95 0.108), a continuous full-frame shimmer-boil that reads closer to hot flicker than to displacement — individual grains churn everywhere at once. The flow_intensity sweep is a null on camera: lo, default, and hi measure identically (0.0912/0.0915/0.0914); do not cast this param as a visible pace control.

## Energy response
Small on top of a high floor: loud adds a touch of brightness (luma 0.380 → 0.398) and motion (0.080 → 0.092), plus the edge streaks. The field is already boiling in the quiet pad-only half — this effect has no calm state on this track.

## Palette
Saturated fire: vermilion, orange, amber, gold flecks on glimpses of near-black between grains. Mid value, near-monochrome heat, dense and opaque.

## Casting notes
Fire, embers, solar surface, sandstorm, sensory overload — pure full-frame energy texture. Cast it for sustained intensity: peaks, drops, rage, heat. It cannot depict a subject, hold a shape, or sit still; even quiet passages boil, so it is wrong for ambient or contemplative scenes as-is. As a layer it is an opaque wall — use it as the base of a hot scene or blended down under a figure effect, never behind fine detail.
