# Array

> Toroidal speaker emitters per audio band — each fires disc-shaped rings of particles outward like sound becoming visible

- type: feedback
- pace param: speed_mult (lo=0.15 default=0.5 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.0035 default=0.0029 hi=0.0025
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.007 / 0.017
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
Five small glowing doughnut discs stacked in a vertical column receding up the frame like runway lights: red at top, amber, green in the middle, then a dim blue and a faint grey-white at the bottom. Each disc has a dark centre hole. In the loud half a haze of ember-orange particles hangs around the lower discs. Everything else — the great majority of the frame — is black.

## Motion character
Essentially static by catalog scale (all variants ≤0.004). The sweep is inverted: lo (0.0035) reads busiest because slow particles linger as a visible spray cloud around the rings; hi (0.0025) is the cleanest and dimmest, particles clearing before they accumulate. No perceptible displacement — the discs hold position and breathe.

## Energy response
Loud half roughly doubles brightness (luma 0.007 → 0.017) and motion (0.0015 → 0.0029): quiet shows thin, sparse particle rings barely above black; loud fills the same five discs into solid glowing toruses and adds the surrounding ember haze. The response is real but takes place entirely inside a dim, small central column.

## Palette
Traffic-light band colours — red, amber, green, blue, white — plus orange ember spray, all deeply saturated points on near-total black.

## Casting notes
Flag: near-black as suspected — luma never exceeds 0.02 in any variant, and the discs occupy a narrow central column of a mostly empty frame. Casts as an accent or votive element: a speaker totem, runway lights, a chakra column, distant beacons. Use lo speed_mult if any particle spray should be visible. Cannot fill a frame or carry a scene; pair over a brighter background layer or keep for intimate, dark passages.
