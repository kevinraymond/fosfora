# Turing

> Reaction-diffusion sculptor — Gray-Scott chemical fields driving particles into organic patterns

- type: feedback
- pace param: sim_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.91) — no hi variant rendered
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.0338 default=0.0324
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.038 / 0.109
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
White organic lacework on pure black. In the quiet half: a scratchy central burst
of white speckles — like frost crystals or mold spores seeding outward, with
faint debris flecks toward the frame edges. In the loud half the field has grown
into a full coral-lace web: branching white filaments netting the whole frame,
wrapped around big smooth black voids like cells or bubbles pressed into the
pattern. Filament edges carry magenta/green chromatic fringes, giving the lace a
faint electric shimmer. Reads as living chemistry — lichen, coral, brain coral,
petri-dish growth.

## Motion character
Growth, not travel: the pattern crawls, thickens, and eats into the black voids
in place — pace reads as churn at the filament edges. Moderate when fed (loud
mean 0.0324, p95 0.074). The sim_speed sweep barely moves the numbers (loud mean
0.0338 lo vs 0.0324 default despite a 0.15-vs-0.91 setting); in stills the lo
variant's web is thinner and sparser, so the dial reads as pattern density more
than visible tempo.

## Energy response
Dramatic: the loud half is 3x brighter (luma 0.038 → 0.109) and 4x busier
(motion 0.0079 → 0.0324). Quiet music leaves scattered seed-speckles on black;
the loud half's onsets and bass grow them into the full frame-filling web. This
is an effect that visibly *accumulates* the music — energy becomes structure.

## Palette
Essentially black and white — bone-white filaments on true black — with thin
magenta/green fringe accents. Dark mean, extreme local contrast, near-zero saturation.

## Casting notes
Organic growth, decay, infection, frost spreading on glass, coral, neural lace.
Its black negative space makes it a clean overlay above colored washes (the
white web picks up any underlying hue) and a striking solo for stark monochrome
passages. It cannot do smooth gradients, recognizable objects, or instant beat
hits — growth takes seconds, so cast it across a phrase, not on a drum hit.
Quiet passages show only sparse speckle; it cannot carry a quiet scene alone.
