# Lattice Shells

> 3D cellular automata — concentric expanding rings, like a cross-sectioned rose (S4-8/B6-8/14/M). Bass drives the wavefront; the per-cell lifetime carves the layers.

- type: particle
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.00479
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.0903 / 0.1269
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A golden-yellow rose of concentric fuzzy shells centered on a midnight-navy
void. Quiet half: an unmistakable yellow rose in cross-section — soft
petal-like rings spiraling into a center, with a small satellite bud beside
it, rendered in fuzzy ochre and cream like felted wool. Loud half: the rose
has inflated into a large round golden orb — a harvest moon whose surface
still carries faint concentric ripple-rings and a mottled, woolly texture.

## Motion character
Slow growth, near-static per frame: quiet motion 0.00049 (frozen), loud
0.0048. Movement reads as an expanding wavefront — new shells swelling
outward from the core in slow motion — plus gentle surface churn. Nothing
darts or flickers. No pace param; bass sets the wavefront tempo.

## Energy response
The strongest bloomer of the Lattice family: quiet-to-loud luma rises 0.090 →
0.127 and contrast doubles (luma_std 0.076 → 0.152) as the rose grows from a
palm-sized blossom into a frame-dominating golden sphere. Motion rises ~10x.
In silence it holds its shape, frozen.

## Palette
Warm golds, ochres and butter yellows — the one warm object in the Lattice
set — against a nearly black navy-violet background. Soft, matte, mid-low
saturation; no hard edges anywhere.

## Casting notes
The prettiest single-object Lattice: casts as a golden rose, a ringed sun or
moon, a slow-blooming flower, tree-ring geology. Suits warm, reverent,
organic-growth, sunrise-swell moods. It is a slow centerpiece — no strobing,
no travel, no full-frame coverage — so use it as a focal hero on darkness or
under faster overlay layers. Pairs naturally with cool-toned accents (Lattice
Pulse's teal specks, Panorama's blue lanes) for complementary contrast.
