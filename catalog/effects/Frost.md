# Frost

> Spectral-flatness material dissolution: tonal sound freezes into faceted crystal, noisy sound melts it into drifting sand

- type: shader
- pace param: drift_speed (lo=0.15 default=0.5 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.101 default=0.101 hi=0.101
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.566 / 0.582
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A frame-filling mosaic of irregular polygonal cells — a Voronoi crackle like river ice, leaded glass, or a crazed ceramic glaze. Cells of glassy ice-blue and pewter with granular, sanded interiors sit in thick bright-white mortar seams; the seams carry faint prismatic red/blue fringes like light through a bevel. In the loud half many cell interiors turn sandy tan-brown and grittier, as if the ice were silting up.

## Motion character
Quiet half: near-still — the mosaic holds, grain gently crawling (motion 0.021). Loud half: a fast full-field shimmer as cells reseed and their sand interiors churn (0.101, p95 0.169) — reads as aggressive grain-flicker across a fixed tile structure, not travel. The drift_speed sweep is a null on camera: lo/default/hi all measure 0.101; do not cast it as a visible pace control.

## Energy response
Motion is the tell, not light: loud multiplies mean Δ five-fold (0.021 → 0.101) while luma barely moves (0.566 → 0.582). Visibly, the calm blue crystal plate of the quiet half becomes a buzzing plate with tan sand grinding inside the cells — the documented crystal-to-sand morph reading as texture change plus shimmer.

## Palette
Cool and bright (luma 0.57): ice blue, slate, pewter cells, white seams, prismatic edge glints; sandy tan intrusions when loud. Low-to-mid saturation, high key, no true darks.

## Casting notes
Ice, frost, stained glass, cracked earth, mosaic tile, crystalline abstraction. A bright opaque full-frame surface — strong as a wintry background or a texture top-layer, hopeless for darkness, focal subjects, or silhouettes. Mind the two regimes: near-still in tonal/quiet passages, near-strobe grain shimmer (p95 0.17) when the mix gets noisy — cast it where that hard flip is a feature, e.g. a drop turning ice to sand.
