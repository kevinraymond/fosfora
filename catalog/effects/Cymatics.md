# Cymatics

> Standing wave patterns — particles form Chladni nodal lines synchronized to audio frequency bands, with rotation and symmetry transforms

- type: particle
- pace param: rotation (lo=0.15 default=0.3 hi=0.9)
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): lo=0.041 default=0.042 hi=0.047
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.055 / 0.081
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A black plate dusted with sparse multicolor pixel grit. In the quiet half: a razor-sharp white X — two crossing diagonal beams meeting in a blazing knot — framed by hairline electric-blue rounded-square contours nested toward the edges. In the loud half the X becomes a six-armed star of white-and-ice-blue beams reaching the frame corners, with blue ellipse loops in the corners and denser confetti sparkle. Laser cross on a vibrating plate.

## Motion character
Particles snap between nodal-line figures: beams swing, patterns lock, then scatter and re-form. Loud-half p95 motion is 0.184 against a 0.042 mean — spiky, near-strobe transitions on onsets rather than continuous churn. The rotation sweep is mild (0.041 → 0.047 lo-to-hi); hi spins the figure faster and runs slightly dimmer (luma 0.074 vs 0.081), same composition.

## Energy response
Quiet-to-loud nearly triples motion (0.0159 → 0.0424) and brightens the plate (luma 0.055 → 0.081): the four-arm X grows to a six-arm star, beams extend full-frame, and onset scatter throws visible dust bursts. Quiet half is a stable, austere figure; loud half is kinetic and flashy.

## Palette
Black dominates (luma 0.05–0.08). White-hot beam cores, electric blue beam falloff and contour lines, and a fine speckle of red/green/violet pixel grit. High contrast, thin-line graphics, almost no filled area.

## Casting notes
Laser shows, oscilloscope/physics-lab imagery, sacred geometry on black, sci-fi HUD moments. It draws precise luminous line-figures on darkness — the negative space is the point — so it layers beautifully over other dark effects but cannot fill a frame with color or texture. Loud-half pattern snaps read strobe-adjacent (p95 0.18): strong for peaks, harsh for ambient scenes. Documented pitch-class mode selection means the figure changes with harmony, not just level.
