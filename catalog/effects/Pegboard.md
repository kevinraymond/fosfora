# Pegboard

> Your image re-made as a backlit peg toy — samples snap to a square lattice, an ordered dither decides which holes carry a peg, colour quantizes to an eight-peg tray, and kicks shower pegs out of the board

- type: particle
- pace param: none
- motion (loud half, mean Δ): default=0.011
- luma (quiet/loud at default): 0.362 / 0.393
- headless caveats: none

## Imagery
A neutral gray board of tiny dimpled holes fills the whole frame; on it, an eagle with
spread wings assembled from thousands of square translucent pegs — white and cream through
the body, gold and olive along the wings, with stray candy-colored specks (green, red,
blue) scattered like knocked-loose pegs around the silhouette. Reads as a Lite-Brite or
airport split-flap board holding one big picture.

## Motion character
Low motion (0.009 quiet, 0.011 loud): the figure holds still and the movement is shimmer —
dither flicker inside the pegs plus individual pegs popping out and drifting off the
silhouette. Reads as flicker and sparse pop, never as displacement of the image itself.
No pace sweep was rendered.

## Energy response
The loud half brightens the peg mass toward white (luma 0.362 → 0.393) and litters more
loose pegs around the figure; motion rises only mildly (0.0087 → 0.0113). Documented
response: bass drives the backlight, kicks pop a fraction of the board out of its holes.

## Palette
Mid-gray board; pegs in white, cream, gold, olive with scattered saturated specks.
Mid-value overall, low saturation with candy accents — a quantized, toy-plastic look.

## Casting notes
BYO-media effect: the catalog render shows a stock eagle test image — whatever image you
feed it becomes the peg mosaic. Casts for retro-toy, mosaic, departures-board, pixel-art
nostalgia; a natural foreground/portrait layer over a dark mover. It cannot do smooth
gradients, fast motion, or abstract wallpaper — it needs a legible source image to matter.
