The app and what it ships with
------------------------------

Fosfora
    The app itself. MIT or Apache-2.0, at your option.
    Copyright (c) 2025-2026 Kevin Raymond
    Source: https://github.com/kevinraymond/fosfora

whisper.cpp 1.8.3 (speech to text, inside the app's library)
    MIT.
    Copyright (c) 2023-2024 The ggml authors
    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp, built from the copy
    in the whisper-rs-sys 0.15.0 crate.

Whisper base.en weights (ggml-base.en.bin)
    MIT. OpenAI's Whisper model, converted to ggml by the whisper.cpp
    project.
    Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI
    Source: https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp

The voice model (s1-17m-int8.onnx, its tokenizer and spec)
    Apache-2.0. Fosfora's own 17M decision model, trained from
    cross-encoder/ettin-reranker-17m-v1 (Apache-2.0), whose tokenizer
    it uses.
    Source: https://huggingface.co/kjraym/fosfora-voice-s1-17m

ONNX Runtime 1.28.0 (libonnxruntime.so, runs the voice model)
    MIT.
    Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation
    Source: the com.microsoft.onnxruntime:onnxruntime-android AAR on Maven
    Central, https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

OpenXR loader 1.1.63 (libopenxr_loader.so)
    Apache-2.0.
    Copyright 2017-2026 The Khronos Group Inc.
    Source: the org.khronos.openxr:openxr_loader_for_android AAR on Maven
    Central, https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenXR-SDK

LLVM libc++ (libc++_shared.so, the C++ runtime whisper.cpp uses)
    Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception, from the Android NDK.
    Source: https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project

"Ember Glow" by oglsdl (audio/ember_glow_excerpt.ogg, seconds 30 to 75)
